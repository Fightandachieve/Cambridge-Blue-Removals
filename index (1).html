How it works

A simple moving process from estimate to completion.

Instant estimate

Enter property size, postcodes, distance, access details, boxes and optional services. Large/specialist items can be added as an optional note.

Clarification call

We contact you to verify access, parking, items, timing and any special requirements.

Pack & prepare

Pack yourself or add professional packing and the combined Dismantling & Reassembly service. Fragile-item requirements can be noted for manual confirmation.

Move day

The team loads, transports and unloads your belongings. Partner cleaning or taxi discounts can be added where eligible.

Unpack & reassemble

Unpacking can be requested for manual pricing, while furniture reassembly is available through the combined Dismantling & Reassembly add-on.

Track progress

If you cannot be present, check booking status and eligible live vehicle location from the tracking page.
