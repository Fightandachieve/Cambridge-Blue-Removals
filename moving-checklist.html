Moving checklist

A simple plan for moving day.

A practical customer resource that can later be expanded into a downloadable PDF.

4 weeks before

Confirm the moving date, book removals, start sorting belongings, identify large/specialist items, check parking/access and begin arranging utility changes.

2 weeks before

Start packing non-essential items, label boxes by room, confirm key arrangements, prepare important documents and decide whether packing or storage help is required.

1 week before

Confirm the removal details, parking/access, furniture disassembly, fragile items, cleaning arrangements and any moving-day taxi requirements.

The day before

Pack an essentials bag, charge phones, separate valuables/medication/documents, empty and prepare appliances where appropriate and keep keys accessible.

Moving day

Walk through the property with the team, point out fragile/large items, keep essential documents with you, check rooms before leaving and use the tracking page if you are not present.

After arrival

Check key items, direct boxes to rooms, confirm furniture placement, record any issues promptly and complete unpacking/assembly services if booked.
